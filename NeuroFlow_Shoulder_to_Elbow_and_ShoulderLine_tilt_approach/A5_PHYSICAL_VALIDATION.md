# A5 seated left-arm physical webcam validation

The single-participant prototype completed anatomical left selection, right-arm isolation, automatic recording, left reference capture, slow and insufficient-elevation diagnostics, independent six-recording practice calibration, and held-out evaluation. The local frozen left threshold is **4.056**; right remains **2.224**. Initial held-out summaries classified **8/8 definitively labeled recordings for each arm**, with uncertain recordings analyzed separately. These results do not establish clinical validity or independent anatomical-angle accuracy.

Use the following sequence to reproduce the physical checks on another setup. Automated tests use a simulated camera and cannot establish physical webcam accuracy. Launch `START_HERE.bat`, select **seated** (option 14 if needed) and **left** (option 16), and keep the raw AVI, timestamps and sidecars together. Use option 2 for live viewing and option 17 for diagnostic captures; do not use held-out test clips to tune settings or thresholds.

1. **Selected left angle:** With the right arm resting, raise and lower the anatomical left arm. Confirm left shoulder (11) and elbow (13) are highlighted on the mirrored preview and the angle progresses from near 0 to near 90 degrees. Briefly hide the left wrist; the shoulder-to-elbow angle should remain usable if those two joints are visible. Check shoulder-line tilt separately.
2. **Arm isolation:** Use option 17 with the left arm resting while moving only the right arm. The left angle and detector must not report a left repetition. This recording stays in `diagnostic/`. Switch to right and confirm the existing right preview and angle still behave as before, then switch back to left.
3. **Reference:** First rehearse in option 17. From a clean left seated setup, use option 4 to record one left raise to approximately 90 degrees and return to rest. Confirm automatic stop after return dwell, metadata `arm=left`, and a video, timestamp CSV, JSON and `.live_pose.jsonl` under the left `reference/` folder. Review this reference before using it for calibration.
4. **Normal return:** Rehearse with option 17, then use option 5 for one normal left repetition. Confirm 500 ms resting baseline, sustained start, confirmed return and automatic termination. Verify that repetition completion and target-reaching score are reported separately.
5. **Slow return:** Rehearse with option 17, then use option 5 for a left repetition lasting longer than 10 seconds. Confirm that it completes on return, without a recording-duration cutoff. Timeout should begin at movement onset and remain 25 seconds unless intentionally reconfigured.
6. **Insufficient elevation:** Rehearse with option 17, then use option 6 for a lower left raise followed by a complete return. It should terminate as a completed repetition while failing the elevation target in scoring.
7. **Replay agreement:** Run reference review (7) and practice scoring (8). Compare the saved capture timestamp/angle/tracking rows and detected boundaries with offline replay. Confirm `measurement_source=live_capture`; AVI reprocessing is diagnostic only. Inspect tracking-loss abstention and missing frames without filling them.

Only after these checks pass on a new setup, collect a fresh left practice set: three complete repetitions at comfortable varied speeds and three insufficient-elevation repetitions that return to rest. Use option 8 to inspect score separation, then option 9 to freeze a **new left-only** threshold. Record independent held-out left clips with option 10 and evaluate with 11; option 12 is the live demonstration after calibration. Preserve the frozen seated right-arm threshold **2.224** and all existing right recordings. Do not use held-out clips for threshold selection. The existing participant-specific left calibration and recordings remain local; they are not part of the A5 source pull request.

Known limitations include partial landmark occlusion and camera-plane projection. Tracking abstention is expected when the selected shoulder or elbow is unreliable. Independent anatomical-angle validation is pending. The image-vertical angle is a 2D projection, not a validated anatomical ROM measure; this is a prototype, not a clinically validated rehabilitation device. Bilateral scoring is outside A5.
